## Product Principles
1. Lead with hands-on project evidence over claims — show the process (design, testing, iteration), not just outcomes.

3. Ground every claim in the resume; never invent metrics, testimonials, or accomplishments.
4. Stay static, fast, and simple to maintain and deploy.
